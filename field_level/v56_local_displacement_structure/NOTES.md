# v56 local displacement structure

Diagnostic-only field-level analysis of the v24 independent-centre residual vectors. It adds nearest-neighbour vector agreement, deterministic checkerboard split-half agreement of 6×6 block means, and value-derived figures. It does not alter registration, masks, sampling, or defaults.

Inputs are the v24 vectors retained on W: and the tracked v24 field summary. Results are stored under `data/results/v56_local_displacement_structure_20261006/`. Known limitation: crossmark centre coordinates are absent; no coordinate is inferred from images or line detection.
